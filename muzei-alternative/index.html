Where each one is better

Muzei if you want art on your home screen as well, softly blurred behind your icons, or your own photos as the source. It is open source and has been around for years.

DailyArt if you want to sit down with one artwork a day and read a longer story about it.

Google Arts & Culture if you want to explore whole museums, exhibitions and virtual tours.

Genius Gallery if you want the art waiting on your lock screen every time you unlock, with one short line that tells you what you are looking at, and big ideas from science, history and philosophy alongside the paintings. Free, with no ads and no account, in ten languages, and on Android TV as an art screensaver.
