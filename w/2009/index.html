Proof from Axioms

Euclid, Elements (Oxyrhynchus papyrus P.Oxy. I 29), -300

A handful of obvious rules, and hundreds of theorems that follow by force.

Euclid started from a handful of self-evident rules and built hundreds of theorems, each forced by the ones before. This scrap, copied about 1,900 years ago, shows the method that made math certain.
