Composition IV

Wassily Kandinsky, 1911

Look again: lances, Cossacks, a castle on a hill, a rainbow.

It looks abstract, but look again: two black lances, Cossacks on horseback, a castle on a blue hill, a rainbow. Kandinsky was erasing the world one step at a time, until only its music remained.
