A Capsule of Asteroid Dust

NASA / Keegan Barber, 2023

Asteroid dust holding 14 of the 20 amino acids life uses, made in space.

On 24 September 2023 this capsule fell into the Utah desert carrying 121.6 grams of asteroid Bennu. Inside, scientists found 14 of the 20 amino acids life uses, made in space, not on Earth.
