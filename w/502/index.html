Paris Street; Rainy Day

Gustave Caillebotte, 1877

A lamppost splits the city in two, and no one looks at anyone.

Look at the lamppost: it splits the painting in two, and no one on either side is looking at anyone. Caillebotte painted the loneliness of the modern city before it had a name.
