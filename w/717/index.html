Metamorphosis insectorum Surinamensium, Plate 7

Maria Sibylla Merian, 1705

A whole life on one branch: egg, caterpillar, cocoon and wings.

Merian put each insect's whole life on a single branch: egg, caterpillar, cocoon and wings together. Showing nature as a cycle rather than a pinned specimen put her decades ahead of the science of her day.
