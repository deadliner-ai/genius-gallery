Aldrin's Bootprint

Buzz Aldrin, 1969

No wind, no rain: a print like this could last millions of years.

Aldrin photographed his own bootprint as an experiment, to see how the lunar soil packs down. With no wind or rain to erase it, a print like this could last for millions of years.
