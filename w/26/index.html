Sistine Madonna

Raphael, 1512

Two bored little angels at the bottom stole the show from the Madonna.

Raphael tucked two bored little angels at the bottom, and they became some of the most reproduced angels on Earth. In 1945 the Soviets took the painting to Moscow for ten years.
