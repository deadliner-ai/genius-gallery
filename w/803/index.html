Boulevard du Temple

Louis Daguerre, 1838

The street was crowded, but only the man getting a shoeshine stood still long enough.

The street was full of carriages and people, but the exposure took minutes, so they all vanished. Except one man who stood still to have his boots shined: the first person ever photographed.
