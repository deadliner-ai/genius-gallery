Snow Crystals by Electron Microscope

USDA ARS / Eric Erbe, Christopher Pooley, 2005

To see a snowflake without melting it, they kept it colder than minus 170.

To image snowflakes without melting them, USDA scientists kept them colder than minus 170 degrees Celsius and scanned them with electrons, revealing steps and facets no ordinary camera could show.
