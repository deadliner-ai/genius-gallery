Thunderstorm Beneath the Summit

Katsushika Hokusai, 1830

Clear sky at the summit, lightning at its feet.

Clear sky at the summit, lightning at its feet. Hokusai shows one mountain in two weathers at once, as if to say the storm is only one layer of what is happening.
