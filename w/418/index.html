Wivenhoe Park

John Constable, 1816

The commission that finally let him marry, after seven years of waiting.

This commission helped Constable finally afford to marry Maria Bicknell, after a seven-year courtship her family opposed. He even widened the canvas to fit in everything the owner wanted.
