John Brown

Augustus Washington, 1846

A hand raised as if swearing an oath, more than a decade before Harpers Ferry.

A Black photographer made this portrait of the abolitionist John Brown, hand raised as if swearing an oath, more than a decade before his raid on Harpers Ferry. Washington later emigrated to Liberia.
