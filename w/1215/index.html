Morning Glory Pool, Yellowstone

NPS / Neal Herbert, 2015

It was once deep blue, until visitors' coins clogged its vents.

Morning Glory was once a deep blue. Visitors threw in so many coins, rocks and trash that they clogged its vents; the water cooled, and orange and yellow bacteria crept in around the rim.
