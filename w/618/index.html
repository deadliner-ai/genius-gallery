Sombrero Galaxy

NASA / Hubble, 2003

A galaxy that looks like a hat, hiding a black hole of a billion suns.

Seen almost exactly edge on, this galaxy looks like a hat. Its bright core hides a black hole of about a billion suns, and its light set out some 30 million years ago.
