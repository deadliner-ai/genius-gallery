Germ Theory: Pasteur's Swan-Neck Flask

Louis Pasteur (drawing from Microbe Hunters, 1926), 1859

Air gets in, dust gets stuck in the bend, and the broth stays clear.

Pasteur boiled broth in a flask with a curved neck: air got in, but dust and microbes stuck in the bend, and the broth stayed clear. Decay is the work of microbes, and so, it turned out, is disease.
