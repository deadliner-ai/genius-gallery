Fine Wind, Clear Morning

Katsushika Hokusai, 1830

Not a mountain, but the few minutes a mountain turns red.

Three colors, almost no detail, and a mountain that turns red for a few minutes on late-summer mornings. Hokusai did not paint a mountain; he painted a moment.
