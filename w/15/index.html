Isenheim Altarpiece

Matthias Grünewald, 1512

Christ covered in sores, so the sick in the hospital would see God suffer like them.

It stood in a hospital for victims of St Anthony's fire, a disease that ravaged the skin. Grünewald covered Christ in sores, so the sick would see God suffer as they did.
