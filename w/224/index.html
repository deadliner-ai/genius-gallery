Deer in the Forest II

Franz Marc, 1914

How does a deer see the world? A forest broken into shards of light.

Marc asked: 'How does a horse see the world, or an eagle, a deer, or a dog?' Here he tries to answer, breaking the forest into shards of light around the resting deer.
