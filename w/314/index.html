Sudden Shower over Shin-Ōhashi Bridge and Atake

Utagawa Hiroshige, 1857

Two sets of rain lines at slightly different angles, so the downpour seems to move.

Look at the rain: two sets of fine lines cut at slightly different angles, so the downpour seems to shift as you watch. On the bridge, everyone runs bent under hats and mats.
