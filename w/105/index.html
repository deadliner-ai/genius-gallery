A Sunday Afternoon on the Island of La Grande Jatte

Georges Seurat, 1884

No mixed colors at all: your eye does the mixing, a few steps back.

Seurat never mixed his colors. For two years he set countless tiny dots side by side and left the mixing to your eye, a few steps back.
