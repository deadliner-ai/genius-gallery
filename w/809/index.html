Dictyota dichotoma (Photographs of British Algae)

Anna Atkins, 1843

No camera at all: real seaweed laid on paper, and the sun did the rest.

No camera at all: Anna Atkins laid real seaweed on light-sensitive paper and let the sun do the rest. Her privately made volumes are often called the first book illustrated with photographs.
