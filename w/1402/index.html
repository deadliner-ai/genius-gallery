Pollen Grains

Dartmouth Electron Microscope Facility, 2004

Every plant's pollen is a fingerprint, and forensic scientists read it.

Sunflower, morning glory, hollyhock, lily: each plant's pollen has its own shape, spikes and pits, as distinct as a fingerprint. Forensic scientists use it to tie a suspect to a crime scene.
