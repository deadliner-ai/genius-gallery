Fissure 8, Kīlauea

USGS / Hawaiian Volcano Observatory, 2018

A crack in a neighborhood that buried 700 homes and made new land.

In 2018 a crack opened in a Hawaiian neighborhood and fountained lava for over two months. Fissure 8 buried more than 700 homes and added about 3.5 square km of new land to the island.
