The Fighting Temeraire

J. M. W. Turner, 1839

A ghost of Trafalgar, towed to its death by a small smoking tug.

A ghostly warship that fought at Trafalgar is towed to the breakers by a small, smoking steam tug. Turner called it his 'darling' and refused to sell it.
