Interior of the Pantheon, Rome

Giovanni Paolo Panini, 1734

A dome already 1,600 years old, and the hole at its top has no glass.

The dome over these tourists was already 1,600 years old when Panini painted them. It is still the largest unreinforced concrete dome on earth, and the hole at its top has no glass.
