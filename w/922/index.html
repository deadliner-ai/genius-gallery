Self-Portrait at Twenty-Eight

Albrecht Dürer, 1500

Face-on, a pose reserved for Christ, 'in everlasting colours'. He was 28.

At 28, Dürer painted himself face-on, a pose until then reserved for images of Christ, and wrote that he made himself 'in everlasting colours'. Few artists ever claimed so much.
