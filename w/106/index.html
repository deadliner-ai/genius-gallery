Woman with a Parasol

Claude Monet, 1875

When he painted a woman with a parasol again, years later, he left her face blank.

This is Camille, Monet's wife, with their son Jean on a windy hill. She died four years later at 32, and when Monet painted a woman with a parasol again in 1886, he left her face blank.
