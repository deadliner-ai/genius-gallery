Muhammad Ali

Ira Rosenberg / New York World-Telegram, 1967

He said no to the draft and lost three and a half years of his prime.

In 1967 Ali refused to be drafted for Vietnam and was stripped of his title, losing three and a half years of his prime. In 1971 the Supreme Court overturned his conviction unanimously.
