The Market Place and the Grote Kerk at Haarlem

Gerrit Berckheyde, 1665

Go to Haarlem today and you can stand exactly where he stood.

Berckheyde painted this square again and again, the way a photographer returns to one corner. Visit Haarlem today and you can stand exactly where he stood; the church has hardly changed.
