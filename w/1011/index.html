Lake Bosumtwi, Ghana

NASA / USGS / Landsat, 2015

A meteorite's crater, where souls are believed to say goodbye to Earth.

Ghana's only natural lake fills a crater left by a meteorite about a million years ago. The Asante believe souls come here to bid farewell to Earth before the afterlife.
