HIV Leaving a Human Cell

CDC / Cynthia Goldsmith, 1984

Photographed the year it was shown to cause AIDS. Today it can be made undetectable.

The green dots are new HIV particles budding from a human immune cell, photographed in 1984, the year the virus was shown to cause AIDS. Today treatment can make it undetectable and untransmittable.
