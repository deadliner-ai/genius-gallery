Cloud Study

John Constable, 1822

On the back he noted the hour and the wind, like a weatherman.

On the back of his cloud studies Constable noted the date, the hour and the wind, like a weatherman. He spent two summers on Hampstead Heath doing almost nothing but 'skying'.
