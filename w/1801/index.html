Albert Einstein

Ferdinand Schmutzer, 1921

Offered a country's presidency, he said he lacked the aptitude to deal with people.

In 1952 Israel offered Einstein its presidency. He declined, writing that he lacked the natural aptitude and experience to deal with people, and that the offer moved and saddened him at once.
