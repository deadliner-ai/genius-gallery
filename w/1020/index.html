Siling Co, Tibet

NASA / USGS / Landsat, 2017

Fed by melting glaciers, it swelled until it became Tibet's largest lake.

Fed by melting glaciers, Siling Co has swelled so much in recent decades that around 2014 it overtook Nam Co as the largest lake in Tibet.
