The Horse in Motion

Eadweard Muybridge, 1878

Does a galloping horse ever fly? Twelve cameras said yes.

Does a galloping horse ever leave the ground entirely? No eye could tell. Muybridge set up twelve cameras, and the answer, yes, was a first step toward cinema.
