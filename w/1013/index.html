Columbia Glacier, Alaska

NASA / USGS / Landsat 8, 2019

Where ice once stood, more than 20 km of seawater now fills the fjord.

Alaska's Columbia Glacier has retreated more than 20 km since the 1980s, one of the fastest glacier retreats ever measured. Seawater now fills the fjord where the ice once stood.
