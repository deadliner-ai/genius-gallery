Portrait of a Man in a Red Turban

Jan van Eyck, 1433

'As I can', says the frame. Many believe he painted his own face in a mirror.

On the original frame he wrote 'Als ich can', as I can, a modest motto and a pun on his own name. Many believe the man in the red turban is van Eyck himself, looking into a mirror.
