Kirifuri Waterfall at Kurokami Mountain

Katsushika Hokusai, 1832

Water like long fingers clawing down the rock, and tiny pilgrims looking up.

Hokusai drew the falls as long fingers of water clawing down the rock, with tiny pilgrims gazing up from below. He made his waterfall series in his early seventies.
