Self-Portrait

Robert Cornelius, 1839

On the back he wrote: 'The first light Picture ever taken'. He may have been right.

In 1839 a Philadelphia lamp maker held still for minutes in front of his own camera. On the back he wrote 'The first light Picture ever taken', and it may be the first photographic portrait ever made.
