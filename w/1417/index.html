A Cluster of E. coli

USDA ARS / Eric Erbe, Christopher Pooley, 2005

Much of what we know about genes, we learned from a humble gut bacterium.

E. coli is one of the most studied living things on Earth. Much of what we know about genes, from how they switch on to how DNA is copied, was first worked out in this humble gut bacterium.
