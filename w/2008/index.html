The Square Root of Two

Yale Babylonian Collection, YBC 7289, -1700

A student's clay tablet: the square root of two, off by under a millionth.

A student drew a square and its diagonal, then wrote their ratio: about 1.414213, off by under one part in a million. Later Greeks proved that no fraction can ever hit it exactly.
