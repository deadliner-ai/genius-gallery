The Eye of the Sahara

NASA / ISS Expedition 30, 2011

It looks like a crater, but no meteorite made it.

It looks like an impact crater, but no meteorite made it. The 40 km Eye of the Sahara is a dome of rock layers that wind and time wore into rings, so big it is easiest to see from orbit.
