Night View of Saruwaka-machi

Utagawa Hiroshige, 1856

Japanese prints almost never showed shadows. Here the full moon casts every one.

Look at the shadows. Japanese prints almost never showed them, but here a full moon throws every figure's shadow onto the street of Edo's kabuki theater district.
