The Sun

Edvard Munch, 1911

For decades, the Nobel Peace Prize was handed out beneath this sunrise.

Munch painted this sunrise over the Norwegian coast for the great hall of the University of Oslo. From 1947 to 1989 the Nobel Peace Prize was handed out beneath it.
