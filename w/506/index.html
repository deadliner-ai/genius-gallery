The Little Street

Johannes Vermeer, 1658

For centuries no one knew which street this was. Tax records on canal dues gave it away.

For centuries no one knew which Delft street this was. In 2015 a historian found it in old tax records of canal dues: Vlamingstraat 40 and 42, where houses still stand.
