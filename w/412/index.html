Snow Storm: Steam-Boat off a Harbour's Mouth

J. M. W. Turner, 1842

He claimed he was tied to a mast for four hours. A critic saw only soapsuds.

Turner claimed he had himself tied to a mast for four hours to watch this storm. A critic called the result 'soapsuds and whitewash'; Turner wished he had been in it.
