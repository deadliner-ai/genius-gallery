Vienna Viewed from the Belvedere Palace

Bernardo Bellotto, 1758

A whole capital spread like a carpet on a long golden afternoon.

Empress Maria Theresa hired Bellotto to paint Vienna, and he gave her this: a whole capital spread like a carpet, seen from the palace garden on a long golden afternoon.
