The Law of Large Numbers

Jacob Bernoulli, Ars Conjectandi, 1713

Ten flips, seven heads. Ten thousand flips, almost exactly half. Casinos thank Bernoulli.

Flip a coin ten times and you may get seven heads; flip it ten thousand times and you will land very near half. Bernoulli proved why, and casinos and insurers have lived off that certainty ever since.
