Mathematics Built from Logic

Whitehead and Russell, Principia Mathematica, 1912

Hundreds of pages to prove 1 + 1 = 2: 'occasionally useful.'

Russell and Whitehead tried to derive all of mathematics from pure logic. It took them hundreds of pages to prove 1 + 1 = 2, and they dryly added: 'The above proposition is occasionally useful.'
