Elizabeth Johnstone, Newhaven Fishwife

Hill & Adamson, 1843

She sold fish, held still for long seconds, and became one of the first faces of working people on film.

Elizabeth Johnstone sold fish in Edinburgh. Around 1845 she held still in the sun for long seconds, and her face became one of the first photographs ever made of working people.
