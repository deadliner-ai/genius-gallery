Night Snow at Kambara

Utagawa Hiroshige, 1833

Only greys, black and white, and still you can hear the silence of snow.

Almost no color, only greys, black and white, and still you can hear the silence that snow brings. Bent travelers trudge uphill as the night swallows the village.
