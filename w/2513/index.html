Mars Samples Waiting for a Ride

NASA / JPL-Caltech / MSSS, 2023

Ten sealed tubes of Mars rock, left on the ground. The ride home was cancelled.

In 2023 Perseverance left ten sealed rock tubes on the ground as a backup cache. The mission meant to bring them home was cancelled in its current form in 2026, so for now they wait on Mars.
