La Grenouillère

Pierre-Auguste Renoir, 1869

Two hungry painters, side by side, and out of that summer came Impressionism.

In summer 1869 Renoir and Monet set up their easels side by side at this riverside bathing spot. Renoir wrote that they did not eat every day; out of that hungry summer came Impressionism.
