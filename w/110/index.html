Luncheon of the Boating Party

Pierre-Auguste Renoir, 1880

The woman cooing at the little dog would later marry the painter.

The young woman cooing at the little dog is Aline Charigot, a seamstress Renoir later married. The man straddling a chair at right is Caillebotte, the painter who bought his friends' work.
