The Four Color Theorem

University of Illinois postal meter (Smithsonian), 1976

Four colors for any map. A computer had to check, and the post office celebrated.

Any map can be colored with four colors so no neighbors match. Suspected since 1852, it was proved only in 1976, the first great theorem checked by computer. Illinois stamped its mail with the news.
