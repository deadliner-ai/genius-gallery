Cloud Inversion in the Grand Canyon

NPS / Michael Quinn, 2010

On rare mornings the canyon fills with cloud like a bowl of milk.

On a few rare mornings cold air sinks into the Grand Canyon and is trapped under warmer air above, so the canyon fills with cloud like a bowl of milk while the rim stands in sunshine.
