Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests, Beijing

Balon Greyjoy / Wikimedia Commons, 2020

28 pillars and not one nail: a building that is also a calendar.

This hall stands on 28 wooden pillars and no nails: 4 for the seasons, 12 for the months and 12 for the traditional hours of the day. After lightning burned it in 1889, it was rebuilt exactly.
