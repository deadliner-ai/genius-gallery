The Great Nebula in Orion

Étienne Léopold Trouvelot, 1875

Before cameras could see nebulae, astronomy needed an artist's eye.

Before cameras could capture faint nebulae, astronomy depended on artists' eyes. Trouvelot drew the Orion Nebula through a telescope; the first photograph of it came only in 1880.
