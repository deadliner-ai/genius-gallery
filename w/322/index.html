Moon Pine, Ueno

Utagawa Hiroshige, 1857

A pine branch that grew into a full circle, so people could see the moon through it.

The pine branch really grew into a full circle, and people came to see the moon through it. A typhoon destroyed it in the Meiji era; in 2012 a new one was trained into the same loop.
