Malaspina Glacier, Alaska

NASA / USGS / Landsat 7, 2000

A glacier spreading like batter on a pan, its stripes folded by every surge.

Malaspina pours out of the mountains and spreads like batter on a pan, about 3,900 square km of ice. The zigzag stripes are rock debris, folded each time one of its feeder glaciers surges.
