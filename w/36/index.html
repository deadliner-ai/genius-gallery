Procession of the Magi

Benozzo Gozzoli, 1459

The Medici rode into the Christmas story, and the painter signed his cap.

The Medici painted themselves into the Christmas story, riding with the Magi through Tuscan hills. Gozzoli joined the crowd too, with his name written on his cap.
