Place Value Beats the Abacus

Gregor Reisch, Margarita Philosophica, 1503

Pen against counting board, and Lady Arithmetic is smiling at the pen.

On the left, a man calculates with written Hindu-Arabic numerals; on the right, a rival pushes counters on a board. Lady Arithmetic favors the pen: with place value, the digits do the work.
