The Tree of Life

Gustav Klimt, 1910

A golden tree in a private dining room, known to most only through designs like this.

This golden tree spirals across a dining room in a Brussels mansion, laid in marble, gold and enamel mosaic. The house is private, so most of us know it only through Klimt's working designs, like this one.
