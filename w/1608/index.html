The Forbidden City, Beijing

Gary Todd, 2009

For nearly 500 years, ordinary people could not walk in.

For nearly 500 years ordinary people could not enter the Forbidden City without permission. Its roughly 980 buildings were home to 24 emperors of the Ming and Qing dynasties.
