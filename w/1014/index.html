A Low over Iceland

NASA / Aqua (MODIS), 2003

Earth's spin coils the clouds like a seashell.

A low pressure system spirals off Iceland. In the Northern Hemisphere, Earth's spin turns the winds rushing toward a low counterclockwise, coiling the clouds like a seashell.
