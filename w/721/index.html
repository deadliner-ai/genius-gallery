Large Sunflower (Hortus Eystettensis)

Basilius Besler, 1613

A newcomer from the Americas, filling an entire giant page.

Sunflowers reached Europe from the Americas only in the 1500s, and here one fills an entire giant page. Besler's 1613 book recorded a bishop's garden in Eichstätt, season by season.
