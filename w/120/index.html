Women Ironing

Edgar Degas, 1884

One irons with all her weight. The other just yawns.

One woman presses down with all her weight; the other yawns and grips a bottle. Degas painted Paris laundresses again and again: the long shifts, the heat, the boredom few other painters noticed.
