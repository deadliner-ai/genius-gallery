Polar Bears on the Beaufort Sea Coast

USFWS, 2005

Hunters of the sea ice, stranded on land as the ice retreats.

Polar bears hunt seals from sea ice, so on Alaska's Beaufort coast, where the ice now retreats farther each summer, more of them are stranded on land. In 2008 the US listed them as threatened.
