Crop Circles of Kansas

NASA / USGS / Landsat 7, 2000

Each circle is one sprinkler arm, drinking an aquifer that refills far too slowly.

Each circle is a field watered by one sprinkler arm turning around a pivot, often about 800 m across. Much of the water comes from the Ogallala Aquifer, which refills far slower than it is pumped.
