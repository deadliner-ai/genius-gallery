Graupel: Snow in a Coat of Ice

USDA ARS / Eric Erbe, Christopher Pooley, 2006

There is a snowflake hidden inside this lump of ice.

A snowflake is hidden inside this lump. It fell through a cloud of supercooled droplets that froze onto it until the crystal vanished, becoming the soft pellets known as graupel.
