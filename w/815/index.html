Unidentified Woman

Southworth & Hawes, 1850

No negative, no copies, no name. Just one silver mirror, and she still seems ready to answer.

A daguerreotype has no negative: each one is a single mirror-polished silver plate. We do not know her name, but the Boston studio of Southworth & Hawes made her look as if she could still answer.
