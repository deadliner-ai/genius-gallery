Gas Hure Lake, Qinghai

NASA / ISS Expedition 71, 2024

A lake with no outlet: its water can only leave by vanishing into the air.

Gas Hure Lake on the Tibetan Plateau has no outlet, so its water leaves only by evaporating. The angular ponds beside it harvest potash, salts rich in potassium, for fertilizer.
