Arnolfini Portrait

Jan van Eyck, 1434

'Jan van Eyck was here, 1434.' And in the mirror, maybe he is.

Above the mirror the painter wrote 'Jan van Eyck was here, 1434'. Look into the convex mirror and you find two tiny figures in the doorway; one of them may be him.
