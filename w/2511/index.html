A Sunshade the Size of a Tennis Court

NASA, 2019

Five layers thinner than a hair: one side near 110 C, the other near minus 233.

Five layers of film, each thinner than a hair, keep the Sun's heat off Webb. One side can reach about 110 C while the telescope stays near minus 233 C, cold enough to see faint infrared light.
