Eagle Returning from the Moon

Michael Collins, 1969

Every human then alive is said to be in the frame, except the one holding the camera.

Michael Collins took this from lunar orbit: Eagle returning from the surface, with Earth behind it. It is often said that every human then alive, except Collins himself, is in the frame.
