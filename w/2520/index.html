An Inflatable Heat Shield

NASA / Greg Swanson, 2021

A heat shield wider than the rocket it rides in. The trick: inflate it.

A heat shield wider than any rocket can still fit inside one if it inflates. In November 2022 this 6 meter shield came back from orbit intact, a way to land heavy cargo, or people, on Mars.
