Asteroid Deflection: Dimorphos Before Impact

NASA / Johns Hopkins APL (DART), 2022

We hit an asteroid on purpose, and its orbit shrank by 32 minutes.

In 2022 NASA's DART probe hit this 160-meter asteroid on purpose and cut its orbit by 32 minutes, the first proof we can nudge a space rock. Europe's Hera is due to arrive in November 2026.
