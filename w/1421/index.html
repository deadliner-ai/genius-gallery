A Blue-Eyed Cicada

USGS Bee Inventory and Monitoring Lab, 2021

Thirteen or 17 years underground, almost always red-eyed. This one came up blue.

Periodical cicadas spend 13 or 17 years underground and almost always have red eyes. Blue eyes like these are a rare mutation, and in 2021, a 17-year emergence year in Maryland, the lab found one.
