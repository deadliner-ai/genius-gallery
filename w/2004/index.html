General Relativity: Starlight Bent by the Sun

The Illustrated London News, 1919 / W. B. Robinson, 1915

An eclipse showed stars beside the Sun, shifted out of place, as he predicted.

Einstein said mass curves space, so starlight passing the Sun should bend. The 1919 eclipse let astronomers see stars beside the Sun, shifted as he predicted. GPS still corrects for relativity daily.
