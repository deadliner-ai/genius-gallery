Composition VIII

Wassily Kandinsky, 1923

Ten years after the storm, the storm became an orchestra.

Ten years after the storm of Composition VII, Kandinsky, now teaching at the Bauhaus, turned to circles, lines and triangles. He saw it as a high point of his work: the storm had become an orchestra.
