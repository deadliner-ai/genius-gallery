Europa Clipper: Searching an Ocean

NASA / JPL-Caltech, 2023

An 8.5 meter arm, reaching for a salty ocean hidden under ice.

This 8.5 meter boom will sense the salty ocean under Europa's ice. Launched in 2024, the probe passes Earth in December 2026 and reaches Jupiter in 2030 to ask if that ocean could host life.
