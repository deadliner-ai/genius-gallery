Lady with an Ermine

Leonardo da Vinci, 1489

She was about sixteen, and the ermine is a double pun.

Cecilia Gallerani was about sixteen. The ermine is a double pun: its Greek name echoes her surname, and a court poet called her lover, the Duke of Milan, 'the white ermine'.
