Actiniae (Kunstformen der Natur)

Ernst Haeckel, 1904

Sea anemones, drawn by a biologist, copied by architects.

These are sea anemones, drawn by a biologist. Haeckel's plates argued that nature is a better designer than any of us, and Art Nouveau architects copied them into buildings.
