Al-Khazneh, Petra

Elif Berra Bursa, 2024

Bullet marks up top: Bedouins believed the urn hid a pharaoh's treasure.

Al-Khazneh was not built but carved straight into the cliff by the Nabataeans some 2,000 years ago. Bullet marks on its top urn come from Bedouins who believed it hid a pharaoh's treasure.
