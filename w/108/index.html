Bridge over a Pond of Water Lilies

Claude Monet, 1899

He did not find this pond. He dug it, then painted it for the rest of his life.

Monet did not find this pond; he dug it, diverting a stream despite neighbors who feared his exotic water lilies would poison their water. Then he painted his own creation for the rest of his life.
