Webb's Golden Mirror

NASA Goddard Space Flight Center, 2016

Too big for any rocket, so the mirror folded up and opened only in space.

A mirror 6.5 meters wide was too big for any rocket, so it was built from 18 gold-coated segments that folded up and opened in space. Webb has worked since 2022 and has fuel for about 20 years.
