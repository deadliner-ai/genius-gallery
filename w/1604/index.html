The Chrysler Building Spire

Carol M. Highsmith, 2007

Built in secret inside the tower, hoisted in 90 minutes, tallest for under a year.

In the race for the world's tallest building, the Chrysler's spire was assembled in secret inside the tower, then hoisted into place in about 90 minutes in 1929. It held the title for under a year.
