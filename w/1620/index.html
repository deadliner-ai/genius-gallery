Hagia Sophia, Istanbul

Julian Lupyan, 2024

Its dome seemed to hang from heaven on a golden chain.

When Hagia Sophia was finished in 537 its dome seemed to float, and a writer of the time said it hung from heaven on a golden chain. It was the largest church on Earth for almost 1,000 years.
