Webb's First Deep Field

NASA / Webb, 2022

A grain of sand held at arm's length, and thousands of galaxies inside it.

This whole image covers a patch of sky the size of a grain of sand held at arm's length. Thousands of galaxies are in it, and some of their light has traveled for over 13 billion years.
