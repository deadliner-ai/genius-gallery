Non-Euclidean Geometry

Tiling: Anton Sherwood, 1830

A geometry where triangles add up to less than 180 degrees, and these tiles don't shrink.

For 2,000 years, space was assumed to follow Euclid. Then Lobachevsky and Bolyai built a consistent geometry where triangles add up to less than 180 degrees. In it, these tiles do not shrink toward the rim.
