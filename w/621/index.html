Plumes of Enceladus

NASA / Cassini, 2009

Fountains from a hidden ocean. Some fall as snow, the rest feeds a ring of Saturn.

These fountains spray from an ocean hidden under the ice of a moon only about 500 km wide. Some of the spray falls back as snow; the rest feeds one of Saturn's rings.
