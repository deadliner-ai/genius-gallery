Among the Sierra Nevada, California

Albert Bierstadt, 1868

Painted in Rome, of a valley that does not quite exist.

Bierstadt painted it in a studio in Rome, stitching sketches into a valley that does not quite exist. The paradise that drew Europe's eyes west was partly an invention.
