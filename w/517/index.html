The Ruins of the Old Kreuzkirche, Dresden

Bernardo Bellotto, 1765

He once painted this city at its most beautiful. Now he paints its ruins.

Prussian cannon wrecked this church in 1760. Bellotto painted the shell as workers pulled it down, the same artist who once painted Dresden at its most beautiful.
