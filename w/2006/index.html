Zero as a Digit

Chaturbhuj Temple, Gwalior (photo: Sarah Welch), 876

Carved in 876, one of India's oldest dated zeros, keeping a place empty.

Zero lets a digit's place carry its value: in 270, the 0 keeps the ones place empty so the 7 means seventy. This inscription in Gwalior, carved in 876, holds one of the oldest dated zeros in India.
