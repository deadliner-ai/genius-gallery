The Laughing Hannya

Katsushika Hokusai, 1831

A hundred ghost prints were planned. Only five are known.

A laughing demon woman bursts out of a moon-round frame, clutching a child. Hokusai planned a hundred ghost prints for this series in his seventies; only five are known.
