Ben-Gurion Brings Truman a Menorah

Abbie Rowe / U.S. National Archives, 1951

He declared the state, then left office to live in a desert hut.

He read out Israel's declaration of independence on 14 May 1948. In 1953 he left the prime minister's office to live in a hut at kibbutz Sde Boker, hoping others would follow him to the Negev.
