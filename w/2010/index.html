Calculus

G. W. Leibniz, Acta Eruditorum, 1684

How fast is a falling stone at this very instant? Two men answered, and feuded.

How fast is a falling stone moving at this exact instant? Calculus answers by slicing change into ever smaller steps. Leibniz published it here in 1684; Newton had his own version, and they feuded.
