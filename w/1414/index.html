Giardia, the Suction-Cup Parasite

CDC / Stan Erlandsen, 2000

First seen in 1681, in a sample of the discoverer's own diarrhea.

This parasite grips the lining of the gut with a disk that works like a suction cup. Antonie van Leeuwenhoek first saw its human cousin in 1681, in a sample of his own diarrhea.
