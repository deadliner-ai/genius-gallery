Summit of Mauna Loa, Hawaii

NASA / ISS Expedition 5, 2002

About 9 km tall from the sea floor, and in 2022 it woke after 38 years.

Mauna Loa is the largest volcano on Earth, rising about 9 km from the sea floor. In 2022 it erupted for the first time in 38 years, starting in this summit caldera.
