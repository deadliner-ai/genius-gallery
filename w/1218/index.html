Aurora over Lofoten, Norway

Johannes Groll / Unsplash, 2017

That green is oxygen glowing some 100 km up, struck by the Sun.

The green of the northern lights is oxygen glowing some 100 to 300 km up, struck by particles from the Sun. Lofoten lies under the auroral oval, so on clear winter nights its sky often comes alive.
