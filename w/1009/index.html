Erebus Ice Tongue, Antarctica

NASA / Terra (ASTER), 2001

A glacier floating on the sea, shedding icebergs from its jagged edges.

The Erebus Ice Tongue juts about 11 km into McMurdo Sound, a glacier floating on the sea. Its jagged edges mark where pieces break away and drift off as icebergs.
