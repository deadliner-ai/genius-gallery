Logarithms

John Napier, Mirifici Logarithmorum Canonis Descriptio, 1614

A trick that turns multiplying into adding, said to double astronomers' lives.

Napier found a way to turn multiplying into adding: look up two numbers' logarithms, add them, look up the answer. Tedious calculations shrank so much that Laplace said it doubled astronomers' lives.
