Total Solar Eclipse of 2017

NASA, 2017

400 times smaller, 400 times closer: a perfect fit, for a few minutes.

The Moon is about 400 times smaller than the Sun and about 400 times closer, so it covers it almost exactly. For a few minutes the corona, over a million degrees hot, comes out.
