Solar Filament Eruption

NASA / SDO, 2012

More than 1,400 km per second. Earth would be a speck beside that arc.

On 31 August 2012 a long filament of solar material erupted into space at more than 1,400 km per second. The whole Earth would be a speck beside that glowing arc.
