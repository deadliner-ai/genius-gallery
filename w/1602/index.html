Main Reading Room, Library of Congress

Carol M. Highsmith, 2009

After the British burned the Capitol, the library was reborn from Jefferson's own books.

The Library of Congress was reborn from Thomas Jefferson's own books, bought in 1815 after the British burned the Capitol. This room, under a dome 49 meters high, opened in 1897.
