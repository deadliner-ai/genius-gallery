Demolition of the Houses on the Pont Notre-Dame

Hubert Robert, 1786

Bridges once carried whole streets. Here the river reappears.

Paris bridges once carried whole streets of houses. In 1786 the city tore them down for light and air, and Robert was there to paint the moment the river reappeared.
