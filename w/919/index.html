An Old Man and His Grandson

Domenico Ghirlandaio, 1490

The painter refused to hide the swollen nose. The child doesn't even notice it.

The grandfather's nose is swollen by a skin disease, and the painter refused to hide it. The child does not notice; he looks up only at the face he loves.
