Pascal's Triangle

Diagram: Dixy52 / Wikimedia Commons, 1654

Each number is the two above it, added. Together they count your choices.

Each number is the sum of the two above it, and together they count choices: row 4 says four coin flips can land 1, 4, 6, 4 or 1 ways. Chinese and Persian scholars knew it long before Pascal.
