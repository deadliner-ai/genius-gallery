Along the River During the Qingming Festival

Zhang Zeduan, 1120

More than 800 people on one scroll, and each is doing something different.

The whole scroll is over five meters long and holds more than 800 people, each doing something different. Emperors guarded it like treasure; it is still called China's Mona Lisa.
