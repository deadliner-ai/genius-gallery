Butterfly Nebula

NASA / Hubble, 2009

Butterfly wings of gas hotter than 20,000°C, fleeing a dying star.

The wings of this butterfly are gas hotter than 20,000°C, fleeing a dying star at more than 950,000 km/h. From tip to tip it spans over two light-years.
