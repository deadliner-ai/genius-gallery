Spider Eating a Bird

Maria Sibylla Merian, 1705

Critics called her bird-eating spider a fantasy. Then someone saw one do it.

Merian painted a tarantula eating a bird, and critics called it fantasy. In 1863 the naturalist Henry Walter Bates reported seeing one do it in the Amazon, and 'bird-eating spider' entered the language.
