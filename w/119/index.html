A Cotton Office in New Orleans

Edgar Degas, 1873

His own family's business, and the first Degas a museum ever owned.

Degas painted his own family's business in New Orleans: his uncle tests cotton in front, his brother reads the paper. In 1878 the museum in Pau bought it, the first Degas any museum ever owned.
