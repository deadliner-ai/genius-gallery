Cannabis in the Vienna Dioscurides

Unknown Byzantine artist, 512

Painted for a Byzantine princess, annotated centuries later in Arabic and Hebrew.

This cannabis plant was painted around 512 for the Byzantine princess Anicia Juliana. Later owners wrote plant names in its margins in Arabic and Hebrew, a sign it stayed in use for centuries.
