Fallingwater, Pennsylvania

Carol M. Highsmith, 2007

He built the house on top of the waterfall, not facing it.

Frank Lloyd Wright put the house on top of the waterfall, not facing it, so the family would live with it. Its daring terraces sagged for decades until hidden steel cables saved them in 2002.
