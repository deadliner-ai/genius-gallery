Space Needle, Seattle

Carol M. Highsmith, 2006

A whole tower whose center of gravity sits just 1.5 meters above the ground.

Built in about 400 days for the 1962 World's Fair, the Space Needle stands on a foundation so heavy that the whole tower's center of gravity sits just 1.5 meters above the ground.
