Fishing at Brooks Falls

NPS / L. Law, Katmai National Park, 2021

A bear may eat 30 salmon a day, and the internet votes for the fattest.

Every July sockeye salmon leap up Brooks Falls in Alaska, and brown bears wait at the top with open jaws. A big bear may eat 30 salmon a day, and each fall the internet votes for the fattest one.
