Salmonella Enteritidis

USDA ARS / Jean Guard, 2012

It can slip inside an egg before the shell even forms.

These rods are Salmonella Enteritidis, a bacterium that can infect a hen's ovary and end up inside an intact egg before the shell forms. That is why eggs should be cooked through.
