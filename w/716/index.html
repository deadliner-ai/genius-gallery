Small Emperor Moth on a Damson Plum

Maria Sibylla Merian, 1679

When many believed insects came from rotting mud, she showed them hatching from eggs.

In 1679, when many still believed insects were born from rotting mud, Merian showed caterpillars on the exact plants they eat, from egg to moth. This is plate 13 of that book.
