Martin Luther King Jr., March on Washington

Rowland Scherman / USIA, National Archives, 1963

The dream was not in the script. A singer called out, and he set his notes aside.

The 'dream' was not in his written speech. Near the end, gospel singer Mahalia Jackson called out, 'Tell them about the dream, Martin,' and King set his notes aside and improvised.
