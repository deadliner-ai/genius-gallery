Lower Falls of the Yellowstone

NPS / Jim Peaco, 2013

Almost twice as tall as Niagara, and sketches of it helped create a national park.

At 94 meters the Lower Falls is almost twice as tall as Niagara. Thomas Moran's sketches of this canyon in 1871 helped persuade Congress to create Yellowstone, the world's first national park.
