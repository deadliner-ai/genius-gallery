The Lava Firehose, Kīlauea

USGS / Hawaiian Volcano Observatory, 2017

A cliff that poured lava into the sea like a firehose for weeks.

For weeks in early 2017 a lava tube on Kīlauea opened in a sea cliff and poured a steady stream of molten rock into the Pacific. This is how Hawaii grows, one blast of steam at a time.
