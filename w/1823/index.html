Althea Gibson

Fred Palumbo / New York World-Telegram, 1956

Wimbledon champion, and still refused hotel rooms.

In 1956 she became the first Black player to win a Grand Slam title, then won Wimbledon twice. Even as champion she was often refused hotel rooms; later she broke the colour line in pro golf too.
