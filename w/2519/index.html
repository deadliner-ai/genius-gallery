Sailing on Sunlight

NASA Langley, 2023

Sunlight pushes on everything it touches. Could it carry a ship with no fuel?

Sunlight pushes on everything it touches, very gently. NASA unfurled this 80 square meter sail in orbit in 2024; a boom bent and it tumbled, but sails like it could one day travel without fuel.
