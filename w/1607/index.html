Gateway Arch, St. Louis

Carol M. Highsmith, 2006

Exactly as wide as it is tall, climbed in tiny egg-shaped capsules.

Eero Saarinen's arch is exactly as wide as it is tall, 192 meters each way. Visitors ride to the top in tiny egg-shaped capsules that tilt to stay level as they climb the curve.
