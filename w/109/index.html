The Magpie

Claude Monet, 1868

Snow full of blue and lilac, and the only true black is one small bird.

The Salon jury rejected it in 1869. Look at the snow: it is full of blue, lilac and yellow shadows, and the only true black in the whole field is one small magpie perched on the gate.
