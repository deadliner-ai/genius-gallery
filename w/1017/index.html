Thomsen River, Banks Island

NASA / USGS / Landsat 8, 2015

A famed canoe trip at the top of the world, with a dozen visitors a year.

The Thomsen River on Banks Island in the Canadian Arctic is a famed canoe trip at the top of the world. In a typical year only about a dozen people visit the park around it.
