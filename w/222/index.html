The Tiger

Franz Marc, 1912

A tiger built of crystal planes, perfectly still and ready to spring.

In 1912 Marc took the sharp planes of Cubism and built a tiger out of them, like a crystal. It lies perfectly still among the rocks, yet every angle of its body is ready to spring.
