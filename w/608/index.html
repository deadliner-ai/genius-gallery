Aurora Australis from the ISS

NASA / ISS, 2010

From the station, auroras ripple beneath your feet.

Auroras glow about 100 to 300 km up, and the space station orbits at around 400. Astronauts look down on them from above, like a curtain of light rippling beneath their feet.
