The Skull Sectioned

Leonardo da Vinci, 1489

A skull opened like a house, searching for the seat of the soul.

Leonardo was 37 when he drew this skull, opened like a house with its front wall removed. He hoped to find inside it the point where all the senses meet: the seat of the soul.
