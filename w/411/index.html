Snow Storm: Hannibal and his Army Crossing the Alps

J. M. W. Turner, 1812

A Yorkshire storm on the back of a letter. 'In two years, you'll call it Hannibal.'

Turner watched a storm over the Yorkshire hills and sketched it on the back of a letter. 'In two years,' he told a friend, 'you will see this again, and call it Hannibal.'
