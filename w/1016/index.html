Alvord Desert, Oregon

NASA / USGS / Landsat 8, 2025

Scientists chase dust devils here because Mars has them too.

Oregon's Alvord Desert is the dry bed of an ice age lake, so flat that dust devils race across it. Scientists study them here because similar whirlwinds sweep the plains of Mars.
