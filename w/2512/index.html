MOXIE: Breathing Mars Air

NASA / JPL-Caltech, 2020

A gold box that made oxygen on Mars: about what a small dog breathes in 10 hours.

This gold box rode to Mars and turned its carbon dioxide air into oxygen, 122 grams over 16 runs, about what a small dog breathes in 10 hours. A big version could fuel rockets home.
