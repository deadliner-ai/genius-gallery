Icescapes of the Anzhu Islands

NASA / USGS / Landsat 8, 2016

Redrawn every day by currents, wind and melt, like a sketch that never ends.

Off Russia's Anzhu Islands, sea ice changes from day to day as currents, winds and melting redraw it. The lines across the ice may be pressure ridges where floes collided.
