Cuckoo Wasp from Kruger Park

USGS Bee Inventory and Monitoring Lab, 2018

There is no blue pigment here. The color is made of light.

This metallic blue has no pigment in it. The color comes from microscopic layers in the shell that reflect light, and when attacked, many cuckoo wasps curl up into an armored ball.
