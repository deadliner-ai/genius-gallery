A Net Full of Zooplankton

NOAA / Matt Wilson, Jay Clark, 2011

Up each night, down at dawn: the largest daily migration on Earth.

These drifting crustaceans link microscopic algae to fish, seabirds and whales. Many rise toward the surface each night and sink at dawn, the largest daily migration on Earth.
