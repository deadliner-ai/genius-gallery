The Periodic Table

Dmitri Mendeleev, Osnovy Khimii, 1869

Where the pattern broke he left gaps, and unknown metals came to fill them.

Mendeleev sorted the elements by weight and saw their properties repeat in rhythm. Where the pattern broke he left gaps and predicted unknown metals; gallium, scandium and germanium soon filled them.
