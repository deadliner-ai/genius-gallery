Frederick Douglass

George Kendall Warren, 1879

He posed more than Lincoln did, on purpose: dignity against caricature.

Douglass sat for more photographs than any other American of his century, Lincoln included. He did it on purpose: to replace racist caricatures with the dignity of a free Black man.
