Milford Sound, New Zealand

Bernard Spragg, 2016

After a storm: hundreds of waterfalls that soon vanish.

Despite its name, Milford Sound is a fjord carved by glaciers. It gets nearly 7 meters of rain a year, and after a storm hundreds of short-lived waterfalls pour down its cliffs.
