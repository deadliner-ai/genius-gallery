Crescent of Houses II

Egon Schiele, 1915

The town drove him out for living with his lover. He kept painting its roofs.

Schiele loved Krumau, his mother's hometown on the Vltava, but in 1911 its residents drove him out for living there unmarried with his lover. He kept painting its crowded roofs anyway.
