Chalk Cliffs on Rügen

Caspar David Friedrich, 1818

Painted the year he married, after a honeymoon on these cliffs.

Friedrich painted it the year he married, after a honeymoon on Rügen. The three figures are often read as the painter, his young wife Caroline and his brother.
