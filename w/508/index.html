The Golden Bend in the Herengracht

Gerrit Berckheyde, 1671

Look at the gaps: the city's most expensive street, still waiting for buyers.

Amsterdam's most expensive street, painted while it was still half empty. Look at the gaps between the mansions: those lots were waiting for buyers who had not yet arrived.
