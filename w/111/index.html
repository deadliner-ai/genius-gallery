Dance at Bougival

Pierre-Auguste Renoir, 1883

The dancer in red watched the painters so closely that she became one.

The dancer in red is Suzanne Valadon, a former circus acrobat turned model. She watched the painters so closely that she became one, the first woman admitted to the Société Nationale des Beaux-Arts.
