Potala Palace, Lhasa

Fumikas Sagisavas / Wikimedia Commons, 2023

Thirteen stories and over 1,000 rooms, at 3,700 meters.

The Potala Palace climbs 13 stories up a hill in Lhasa, at about 3,700 meters, with more than 1,000 rooms. It was the Dalai Lama's winter home until the 14th fled to India in 1959.
