View from the Window at Le Gras

Nicéphore Niépce, 1826

An exposure so long that the sun lights both sides of the courtyard.

The oldest surviving photograph taken with a camera needed an exposure so long, perhaps days, that the sun lights both sides of the courtyard. Then it was lost for half a century, until 1952.
