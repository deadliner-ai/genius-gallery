Delicate Arch under the Milky Way

NPS, 2019

The sky here is protected too, so the Milky Way still blazes above the arch.

Delicate Arch stands 16 meters tall on a bare sandstone rim, and Utah puts it on license plates. Arches became an International Dark Sky Park in 2019, so the Milky Way still blazes above it.
