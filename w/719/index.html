Rosa centifolia (Les Roses)

Pierre-Joseph Redouté, 1817

He painted roses for Marie Antoinette, survived the Revolution, then painted for Joséphine.

Redouté, 'the Raphael of flowers', painted for Marie Antoinette and then for Empress Joséphine, surviving the Revolution in between. Many of his roses grew in Joséphine's garden at Malmaison.
