War Office official photographer / Imperial War Museums, 1942

The V sign began as chalk on occupied walls. He made it his own.

In January 1941 a Belgian broadcaster on the BBC proposed V for Victory as a sign that occupied Europe could chalk on walls. Churchill made it his own, here in Bradford in 1942, cigar and all.
