Lena River Delta, Siberia

NASA / USGS / Landsat 7, 2000

Hundreds of channels, frozen seven months a year, until May sets them loose.

The Lena splits into hundreds of channels on its way to the Arctic Ocean. Its delta, about 32,000 square km, lies frozen some seven months a year, then in May it bursts back into wetland.
