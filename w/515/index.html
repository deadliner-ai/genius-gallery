Dresden from the Right Bank of the Elbe Below the Augustus Bridge

Bernardo Bellotto, 1748

When the city burned, his precise views became its memory.

Bellotto was Canaletto's nephew and signed with his uncle's name. When Dresden burned in 1945, his precise views became precious records of the city that was lost.
