Studies of the Fetus in the Womb

Leonardo da Vinci, 1511

The baby is human. The placenta around it comes from a cow.

The baby is human, but the placenta around it is drawn from a cow. Leonardo, dissecting whatever he could get, joined two bodies in one of the most tender drawings in science.
