Foreign Ferns

Anna Atkins, 1853

That blue is pure chemistry, and in her hands botany turned into poetry.

The blue is the chemistry of the cyanotype, invented in 1842 by Sir John Herschel, a friend of Atkins' father. Within a year she was using it to turn botany into something close to poetry.
