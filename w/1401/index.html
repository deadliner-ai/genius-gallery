Portrait of the Coronavirus

CDC / Alissa Eckert, Dan Higgins, 2020

A virus 100 nanometers wide, painted with red spikes so we could finally see it.

Two CDC medical illustrators built this portrait in January 2020 and painted the spikes red to make a virus 100 nanometers wide feel real. It became one of the most seen images of the pandemic.
