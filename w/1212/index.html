General Sherman

Daniel G. Rego, 2015

About 2,200 years old, and each year it still grows a good-sized tree's worth of wood.

By volume General Sherman is the largest single-trunk tree on Earth, about 1,487 cubic meters of wood. It is roughly 2,200 years old and still adds enough wood each year to make a good-sized tree.
