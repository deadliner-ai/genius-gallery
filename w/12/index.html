Melencolia I

Albrecht Dürer, 1514

A magic square that adds up to 34 every way, with the year hidden inside.

The magic square on the wall adds up to 34 in every direction, and its bottom row hides the year: 15 14. Even genius, here, cannot think its way out of gloom.
