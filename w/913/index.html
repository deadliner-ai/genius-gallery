Queen Mother Pendant Mask: Iyoba

Unknown Edo artist, Benin, 1520

Worn at a king's hip, it is believed to show the queen mother who raised armies.

Carved in ivory for a king of Benin to wear at his hip, it is believed to show Idia, the queen mother who raised armies for her son. British troops took it in the looting of 1897.
