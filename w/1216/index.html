Wisdom, the Oldest Known Wild Bird

USFWS / Jon Plissner, 2023

Banded as an adult in 1956, and at about 74 she laid another egg.

Wisdom the Laysan albatross was banded on Midway in 1956, already an adult. Scientists believe she is the oldest known wild bird, and in late 2024, at about 74, she laid yet another egg.
