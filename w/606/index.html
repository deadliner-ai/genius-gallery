Hubble Deep Field

NASA / Hubble, 1995

Ten days staring at an empty patch of sky. It held about 3,000 galaxies.

In 1995 Hubble stared for ten days at a speck of sky near the Big Dipper that looked empty, a gamble some feared would waste its time. It held about 3,000 galaxies.
