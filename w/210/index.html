Starry Night

Edvard Munch, 1893

The silent sibling of The Scream: no figure, no cry, only breathing darkness.

Painted in 1893, the year of The Scream, this is its silent sibling: no figure, no cry, only the shore at Åsgårdstrand, a few stars and a darkness that seems to breathe.
