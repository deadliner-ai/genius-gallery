Eye of a Resin Bee

USGS Bee Inventory and Monitoring Lab, 2020

Thousands of tiny lenses, and a world of ultraviolet we cannot see.

Each of the thousands of tiny facets in this bee's compound eye is a separate lens with its own narrow view. The bee sees a mosaic, and it also sees ultraviolet patterns on flowers that we cannot.
