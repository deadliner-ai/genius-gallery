L'Absinthe

Edgar Degas, 1875

Two friends posed as lost drinkers, and their reputations paid for it.

The two lost drinkers are Degas' friends posing: the actress Ellen Andrée and the engraver Marcellin Desboutin. When the painting caused a stir in England in 1893, their reputations suffered for it.
