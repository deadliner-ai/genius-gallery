Metamorphosis insectorum Surinamensium, Plate 11

Maria Sibylla Merian, 1705

The planters mocked her for looking for anything but sugar.

In Suriname, Merian wrote, the plantation owners mocked her for looking for anything in their land except sugar. She stayed two years, raising caterpillars and drawing them alive.
