Kinryūzan Temple, Asakusa

Utagawa Hiroshige, 1856

The giant red lantern hangs right over your head.

The giant red lantern of the Thunder Gate hangs over you, as if you were standing beneath it. Beyond, snow covers Sensō-ji, said to be Tokyo's oldest temple, founded in 628.
