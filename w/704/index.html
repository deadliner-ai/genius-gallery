Galileo's Moon (Sidereus Nuncius)

Galileo Galilei, 1610

Mountains on a body everyone believed was perfect.

Left, what Galileo drew in 1609 through a telescope he built himself; right, a photograph. He saw mountains and craters on a body everyone believed was perfect, and the heavens were never perfect again.
