Farm Garden with Sunflowers

Gustav Klimt, 1907

Framed through a telescope, a garden flattens into a wall of flowers.

Klimt spent his summers on Lake Attersee and painted about fifty landscapes, nearly all square. He often framed them through a telescope or opera glass, which flattens a garden into a wall of flowers.
