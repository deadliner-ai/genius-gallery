The Blue Rider

Wassily Kandinsky, 1903

Years before the group had a name, this little rider was already crossing the meadow.

Years before Kandinsky and Franz Marc named their group The Blue Rider, this small rider was already crossing a meadow. Kandinsky later explained: we both loved blue, Marc loved horses, I loved riders.
