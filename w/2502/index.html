Fusion Ignition: Inside the NIF Chamber

LLNL / U.S. Department of Energy, 2006

For the first time, a fuel pellet gave back more energy than the lasers put in.

In 2022 lasers in this chamber made a fuel pellet release more energy than they put in, a first; by 2025 the gain passed 4. Fusion could bring clean, abundant power, but plants are years off.
