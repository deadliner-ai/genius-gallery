An art screensaver for your TV

On Android TV, Genius Gallery is a calm, full-screen gallery: famous paintings, photographs and big ideas, one at a time, browsed with the remote. It can also be the screensaver that appears when the TV sits idle.

Open it as a gallery

  1. Install Genius Gallery from Google Play on the TV. You can also install it from your phone and choose the TV as the device.
  2. Open Genius Gallery from the apps row. The works change on their own; use the remote to move between them.

Make it the screensaver on Android TV

On many Android TV devices: Settings, then Device Preferences, then Screen saver, and choose Genius Gallery. The menu names differ a little between makers.

On Google TV, one extra step

Since Android TV 12, Google TV devices no longer let you pick another app's screensaver in the settings. The common workaround, the same one other screensaver apps use, is one command from a computer connected to the TV with ADB (Android Debug Bridge):

adb shell settings put secure screensaver_components dev.ozk.gallerydream/.ArtDream

To use ADB you first turn on developer options and network debugging on the TV. If that sounds like too much, opening Genius Gallery from the apps row gives you the same art, full screen.

Get it on Google Play

Choosing Ambient mode again in the TV's settings should bring Google's own screensaver back.
