Questions

Is Genius Gallery free?

Yes. The app is free, with no account and no ads. Your likes and settings stay on your phone.

How do I put art on my Android lock screen?

Install Genius Gallery from Google Play and tap Start. From then on a new artwork with one line about it waits on your lock screen every time you unlock. You can turn it off in the settings at any moment.

Does a changing lock screen wallpaper drain the battery?

It is a still image, not a live animation: the app swaps one picture when you unlock and does nothing in between.

Where do the artworks come from?

From museums, libraries and agencies that opened their collections, and from Wikimedia Commons. To the best of our knowledge every work is in the public domain or openly licensed. If you spot a mistake, write to support@deadliner.ai.

Which languages does it speak?

Ten: English, Hebrew, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Russian, Arabic, Chinese and Japanese. Every title, caption and story is translated, not only the menus.

Does it work on Android TV and on iPhone?

On Android TV it is a calm full-screen art screensaver you browse with the remote. On iPhone there is no app yet, but the same works can change on your lock screen through Apple Shortcuts.

The collection includes classical nudes, as art always has. They stay hidden unless you choose to show them and say you are 18 or older.

To the best of our knowledge, the works are in the public domain or openly licensed. Thanks to Wikimedia Commons and to the museums, libraries and agencies that opened their collections to everyone. Found a mistake? Write to support@deadliner.ai.
