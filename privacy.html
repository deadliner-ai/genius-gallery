Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026 · Genius Gallery for Android and Android TV · Published by DEADLINER AI LTD

The short version

DEADLINER AI LTD does not operate an account, analytics or advertising service for this app. Your gallery preferences are processed on your device. Artwork downloads contact Wikimedia as described below.

What stays on your device

Your settings, likes, dismissed works and preference scores derived from viewing time are stored in the app on your device to personalize recommendations. We do not receive these preferences. Recently downloaded images and cards you create for sharing are cached locally. Clear the app storage in Android settings to reset this information. Android backup or device transfer may retain or restore app settings according to your device and Google account settings; uninstalling does not necessarily delete such backups or copies you shared with other apps.

Network requests

To show artwork, the app downloads images directly from Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org and thumb.wikimedia.org), which hosts the public-domain and openly licensed works in the collection. Like any website you visit, Wikimedia's servers receive your device's IP address and a technical request header that identifies the app. We do not receive or see this information. Wikimedia's own privacy policy applies to those requests: foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Internet: to download artwork images.
  • Set wallpaper: to place the artwork on your lock screen, only if you turn this feature on.
  • Run at startup: to keep your lock screen schedule after the phone restarts.
  • Notifications: to show the active lock-screen background service notification when permitted.
  • Foreground service: used only in the "every time the screen turns off" mode, to notice when the screen turns off and change the artwork.

Sharing

When you tap share, the app hands the current image and its caption to the Android share menu. Where it goes next is your choice; the app itself sends nothing.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

תקשורת ברשת

כדי להציג יצירות, האפליקציה מורידה תמונות ישירות מוויקישיתוף (upload.wikimedia.org ו-thumb.wikimedia.org), שם שמורות היצירות שבנחלת הכלל וברישיון פתוח. כמו בכל אתר, השרתים של ויקימדיה רואים את כתובת ה-IP של המכשיר ואת זיהוי האפליקציה. המידע הזה לא מגיע אלינו. על הבקשות האלה חלה מדיניות הפרטיות של ויקימדיה.
