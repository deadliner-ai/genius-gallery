Press kit

Genius Gallery is a free Android app that puts a new masterpiece on your lock screen every time you unlock, with one line about it.

Facts

  • What: art and big ideas on the Android lock screen, a new one at every unlock, each with one short line and the full story.
  • Collection: about 7,000 works and ideas in 24 collections, from Van Gogh and Hokusai to the universe, psychology and history.
  • Languages: ten (English, Hebrew, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Russian, Arabic, Chinese and Japanese), with every caption translated.
  • Price: free. No ads, no account, no in-app purchases. Likes and settings stay on the phone.
  • Devices: Android phones and tablets, and Android TV as an art screensaver.
  • Sources: museums, libraries and agencies that opened their collections, and Wikimedia Commons. To the best of our knowledge every work is in the public domain or openly licensed.
  • Launched: November 2026 on Google Play.
  • Made by: DEADLINER AI LTD, Israel.
